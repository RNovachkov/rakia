Privacy Policy

Rakia for iOS · Last updated 17 September 2026

The short version. Rakia does not collect any data about you. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party code in the app. Your measurements stay on your device and, if you turn on iCloud, in your own private iCloud, which only you can open.

Who this policy is from

Rakia (“the app”) is developed by [YOUR NAME OR COMPANY] (“we”, “us”). You can reach us at [YOUR SUPPORT EMAIL]. For the purposes of the GDPR we are the data controller for the app, although as set out below we do not collect or receive any personal data through it.

What we collect

Nothing. We do not collect, receive, store, sell, share or transmit any personal data. The app contains:

  • No analytics or usage tracking
  • No advertising and no ad networks
  • No crash or diagnostic reporting services
  • No third-party SDKs, libraries or frameworks of any kind
  • No accounts, sign-in or registration
  • No access to your contacts, photos, location, camera, microphone, calendar or health data
  • No embedded web browser and no web content

We operate no servers. There is no address to which the app could send your information.

What the app keeps on your device

The app stores the following locally on your device:

  • The measurements you choose to save — density, temperature, the calculated sugar content and the date you give them
  • Your preferences, such as metric or imperial units
  • Two counters used to decide when to show the App Store rating prompt, and a flag recording that it has been shown

None of this leaves your device except through iCloud sync, described below. Deleting the app removes all of it.

iCloud sync

If you are signed in to iCloud, your saved measurements sync between your own Apple devices using Apple’s CloudKit service, in a private container (iCloud.com.novachkov.rakia).

This data lives in your private iCloud database. It is tied to your Apple Account and only you can reach it. We have no access to it. Apple does not give developers the ability to read the contents of a user’s private CloudKit database, and we hold no key, credential or console access that would let us do so. We could not read your measurements if we wanted to.

Data held in iCloud is processed by Apple under Apple’s Privacy Policy. You can stop syncing at any time by turning iCloud off for Rakia in Settings.

Push notifications

The app includes the push notification capability, but it is used only by Apple’s CloudKit service to signal that your measurements have changed on another of your devices so that the app can sync. We do not send push notifications, we operate no notification server, and we cannot send you messages. The app never asks permission to display notifications to you.

The rating prompt

After you have saved a number of measurements, the app may ask iOS to show the standard App Store rating prompt. The system presents it. We do not learn whether you saw it, dismissed it or left a review, and nothing is sent to us.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children. Its age rating reflects that it concerns the production of alcoholic drinks, not the handling of personal information.

Your rights

Under the GDPR and similar laws you have the right to access, correct, export and erase personal data held about you. Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to retrieve, correct or erase. You stay in full control of what is on your device and in your iCloud: you can view, edit and delete any saved measurement inside the app, and remove all of it at once by deleting the app.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will appear at this address with a new date at the top. Because the app collects nothing, we do not expect material changes. We will not begin collecting anything without publishing a revised policy first.
